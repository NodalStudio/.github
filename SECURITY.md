# Security policy

Security is one of our core practices — we would rather hear about a problem than have it
stay hidden. Thank you for helping keep our projects and their users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public issues, discussions or pull
requests.**

Instead, either:

- use GitHub's **[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)**
  (the *Report a vulnerability* button in the *Security* tab of the repository), or
- email **hello@nodalstudio.io** with the subject line `SECURITY`.

Please include as much of the following as you can:

- the affected repository, version or commit
- the type of issue (for example injection, authentication bypass, data exposure)
- step-by-step instructions to reproduce it, and a proof of concept if you have one
- the impact you expect an attacker could achieve

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix the issue.
- We coordinate the disclosure with you and credit you, if you wish, once a fix is
  available.

We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public disclosure, and
that you avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption while
researching.

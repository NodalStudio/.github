# Support

- **Questions about a project** — open a discussion or an issue in the relevant repository.
- **Bug reports and feature requests** — use the issue templates of the repository.
- **Security issues** — follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md); never report them publicly.
- **Working with us** — for projects, missions or partnerships, write to
  **[hello@nodalstudio.io](mailto:hello@nodalstudio.io)** or visit
  [nodalstudio.io](https://nodalstudio.io).
